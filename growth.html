The problem

A local service business competing for the same customers as every other shop on the island, with a budget too small to absorb waste. Reach alone is worthless here. A view from outside the service radius costs the same as one from a customer three miles away, and it's worth nothing.

The approach

Tight service-area targeting kept spend inside the radius he can actually drive to. I set up the Pixel and Conversions API first, so bookings were measured instead of estimated. The content was built for retention: the hook and the pacing carry the cost per click, not the bid.

The outcome

Over 1,000 link clicks on under $200 of spend, 10 booking-form conversions, and 4 bookings of a $400 service. A separate $85 Messenger campaign brought in 16 leads. The 40-second reel held 20% of viewers to the end.
