The project

Our team designed a pressure vessel to house an acoustic modem on a Navy Wave Glider, an uncrewed surface vehicle that rides ocean waves for long missions. The housing had to survive depth and add as little drag as possible.

What I did

  1. Design. Designed components in SolidWorks.
  2. Prototype. 3D printed prototypes to check fit and form early.
  3. Simulate. Ran CFD in ANSYS and iterated the shape to cut drag by 80%.
  4. Validate. Validated the final design by hydrostatic testing at pressures equivalent to 350m depth.
